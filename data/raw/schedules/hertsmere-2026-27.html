Charges

Your Council Tax Charge

Your Council Tax bill is made up of:

  • A charge from Hertsmere Borough Council
  • A charge from Hertfordshire County Council
  • A charge from the Police and Crime Commissioner
  • Your local parish or town council's charge (if applicable)

You can find your total charge below, broken down by area and valuation band.

Hertfordshire County Council Charge

Hertfordshire County Council will spend more than £1.2 billion delivering services to residents and businesses across the county in 2026/27. The County’s budget for 2026/27 includes a 4.99% increase, which is broken down as:

  • 2% for Adult Social Care
  • 2.99% for general expenditure

Police and Crime Commissioner for Hertfordshire

The charge for the Police and Crime Commissioner has increased by £14 for a Band D property compared to the previous year.

Hertsmere Borough Council

Hertsmere Borough Council’s share of your council tax is 9% of the total. The charge has increased by 2.99%, which is £6.28 for Band D properties compared to the previous year.

Please read Your Council Tax 2026/27 for more information.

Parish/Town Council Charges

Parish or Town Councils set their own rates. This charge only applies if you live in an area with a parish or town council. Residents Bushey, Potters Bar, and Meriden are unparished and therefore do not pay a parish or town council charge.  For more information, please contact your Parish / Town Council.
