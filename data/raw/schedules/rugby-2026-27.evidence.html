How much is my council tax?

The amount of council tax charged depends on the parish or town that you live in. Browse our records below, or search by your parish or town to find out how much each Council Tax band is where you live. The records show the Council Tax charge for 2026-27.

Rugby Borough Council keeps just 7p from every pound of council tax collected to deliver core services. Around 76p in every pound goes to Warwickshire County Council for services such as highways, schools and social care, and around 13p goes to Warwickshire Police. The remainder goes to parish councils which charge a precept or the Rugby Borough Council Town Area special expense.
