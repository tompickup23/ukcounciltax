Bands and charges

You can find out how much Council Tax you will pay each year by finding out the  Council Tax band of your property.

The band of your property is also shown on the bill we send to you, just below where it says: ‘Council Tax demand’. 

Once you know the band, you can then look up the cost against the band below.

More about your Council Tax band

The Valuation Office Agency (VOA) maintains the Council Tax Valuation List. This includes placing new properties within a Council Tax band and changing bands for properties when necessary. 

Understand why your property is in a certain band.

Can I appeal against my property’s band?

Find out more about when you can challenge your band and what you need to do

If you challenge your band, you must continue to pay Council Tax at your current band until your appeal is decided.

You can contact the VOA at GOV.UK. If you are unable to use the online service, you can also contact the VOA on 03000 501 501.

You can make an appeal without instructing an agent or other organisation that may charge you a commission or fee.

How much Council Tax will I pay for 2026/27? 

You can find out how much Council Tax you will pay each year by checking the valuation band of your property, which is based on its value in 1991.

The valuation rates are:  

  • Band A – Up to £40,000  
  • Band B – £40,001 to £52,000  
  • Band C – £52,001 to £68,000  
  • Band D – £68,001 to £88,000  
  • Band E – £88,001 to £120,000  
  • Band F – £120,001 to £160,000  
  • Band G – £160,001 to £320,000  
  • Band H – More than £320,000  

The band of your property is also shown on the bill we send to you, just below where it says: ‘Council Tax demand’.

Long-term empty property premium and second home premium

If you own a property that is empty long-term, you will be charged an extra premium on top of your normal Council Tax depending on how long the property has been empty:

  • empty for longer than 12 months, up to five years, 100% premium is added
  • empty for longer than five years, up to 10 years, 200% premium is added
  • empty for longer than 10 years, 300% premium is added

The council implemented a 100% Second Home Premium with effect from 1 April 2025. For information on exclusions to Premiums, please download the document below.
