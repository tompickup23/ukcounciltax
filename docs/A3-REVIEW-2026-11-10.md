## Decisions taken

* Keep main's navigation and Asylum Stats exclusion. Import the merged analytics test fix by rebase; make no local edit to that test.
* Follow section 5 for locator geometry, reading the focal element's own SVG view box. Keep the selected-band print assertions intact.
* Preserve the five published history amounts with pence. The general whole-pound description in the brief cannot replace the precision in the held source.
* Remove the unused history section and its own-share series as instructed. The current council share remains in Who sets the charge.
* Keep default BandSelector and DataDrawer behaviour, budget route sources and all displaced action targets. Budget pages do receive the accepted shared `ChartActions` change described above. Keep all original statistical data, reorganisation text and the vendored kit unchanged.
* Preserve the verbatim charging-body titles. The text gate's existing policy allows them; the pinned design checker reports Manchester's two source-title hyphens as an advisory finding. Neither its rules nor tolerances were changed.
* The original vertical bars partly covered the Band D value label. The review follow-up above resolves this with the kit's horizontal orientation; no kit edit or tolerance change was needed.
* Keep captures and raw review artifacts ignored under `output/playwright/a3/`; attach them to the PR instead of committing image binaries.
