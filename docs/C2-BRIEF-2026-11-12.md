# C2 recommendation and proposed brief | 12 November 2026

Decision requested before implementation: keep the existing guessing game and C1b fixed-total mode. Develop a separate, labelled freeze-counterfactual mode for the 280 candidate billing authorities only after its data and accounting gates pass. Do not replace C1b or build a national simulation of achievable savings, reserves, demand shocks or future deficits from the present data.

The research and reproducible audit are in `C2-SOURCE-NOTES-2026-11-12.md` and `C2-DRY-RUN-2026-11-12.json`. They show why the earlier easy-mode outline needs tightening.

## Proposed experience

1. Choose the billing council. In a two-tier area offer the county as a separate authority with its own budget and services; never aggregate or apportion them. Keep each authority's edition and source visible.
2. Read a short baseline: the published signed net service totals and financing, plus an explicit description of what this exercise leaves fixed. Missing returns get the existing honest notice.
3. Start from the prior annual Table 8 own precept, excluding parish, with the current published tax base fixed. The starting difference is (current own precept minus prior own precept) times that base. Say explicitly that this is a freeze counterfactual holding the rest of the published budget fixed, not the council's actual deficit. Offer a council-tax control with the correct year-specific referendum guidance. Show Band D only; do not derive other bands.
4. Choose changes in net service spending with numeric inputs and keyboard-accessible sliders. Keep the published baseline separate from the freeze scenario. Allow the reader to restore the published precept and zero service changes, which must restore a zero difference. Allow increases and decreases symmetrically. Keep negative net-income groups separate and fixed for the first version. Do not call a chosen reduction an achievable efficiency or imply service outcomes.
5. Show the scenario difference as changes in spending minus changes in financing. Zero means this arithmetic balances under the stated assumptions, not that a council has approved it or statutory duties are met. Keep the published ledger intact beside the scenario.
6. Offer a copyable summary with authority, year, assumptions, changed inputs, remaining difference, formula version and sources. Put `User requested scenario` on every scenario figure and image. No score rewarding cuts, party references, submission to a council, tracking of choices or persistent storage.

The title and product wording should say what the mode really does. The dry run verifies the necessary annual-table joins. It does not establish practical deliverability, legal feasibility or a future deficit.

## Required implementation gates after approval

- Import and pin any new source before presentation work; preserve original bytes, hashes, edition, displayed precision and null markers.
- Retain the audited annual Table 8 inputs and current Table 10 tax base. Reconcile own, parish, area and tax-base definitions; expose discrepancies. Never substitute the rounded historical series or a total household bill.
- Verify every selected authority's ledger. Exclude the nine missing returns; do not fill from national estimates. Seven RA note 4 authorities require resolution before a cross-source financing scenario. Keep note 8 next to affected business-rate values.
- Map each authority's legal category and exception to the final report before displaying referendum guidance. Test both sides of every strict or inclusive threshold. Do not treat a trigger as a hard legal cap or assume later-year rules.
- Use decimal or integer money arithmetic. Preserve precision internally, round only for display, publish formulas and distinguish calculations from observations. Test zero change, increase, decrease, reversal, missing inputs, negative net lines and nonzero remaining differences.
- No reserves, delivery-rate, unit-cost, shock, statutory-floor or future-year controls without additional evidence and a separate reviewed brief.
- Full site, source, accessibility, CSP, number-difference and both-theme phone/desktop checks. The static published tables must remain available without JavaScript.

## Recommendation for the queue

C1b is useful now: it explains a fixed total without pretending to establish a feasible local budget. Keep it. Complete the picker, comparison, image-weight and shared-kit work independently. The C2 decision should not block those improvements.

Recommended next decision: approve the narrow freeze-counterfactual specification in principle, with a pinned referendum-rule mapping and the accounting gates before construction. Alternatively, retain the current educational modes and stop here. No C2 site code has been built in this research change.

## Remaining source work and boundaries

The 280 count is a candidate count, not a promise that every row is ready to ship.
A first implementation must audit detailed notes, thresholds and component
reconciliation for each selected authority. Start with billing-authority budgets;
the county option needs its own prior-year precept join and rule mapping before
it becomes interactive. A district may still show the existing separate county
learning round. No future-year, population or economic assumption is smuggled
into the current-year tax-base calculation.
