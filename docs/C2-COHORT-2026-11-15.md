# C2 cohort | source review completed 15 November 2026

This completes stages 1 to 3 of the local pilot brief. It supersedes the two
council selection in draft PR 68. It does not authorise publication. The only
playable output is the separate noindex build in `.ops/c2-dist`.

The audit admits 197 of 296 billing authorities: 41 of 63 unitary authorities,
28 of 36 metropolitan districts, 4 of 13 inner London authorities, 10 of 20
outer London authorities and 114 of 164 shire districts. Counties are excluded
from the exercise. All source bytes are the same seven pinned files as the
original pilot. No charge, tax base, budget figure or source edition is changed.

`data/pilots/budget-freeze-eligibility.json` records all 296 decisions, physical
spreadsheet cell addresses, the original printed values, component differences,
full authority-specific notes, review decisions and referendum citations.
`data/pilots/budget-freeze-referendum.json` records the edition, exact comparisons,
GSS membership keyed by edition and quotations checked against `pdftotext -layout` output.
`node scripts/audit-c2-pilot.mjs --check` reconstructs both files and the playable
fixture from the raw bytes and fails on drift. The old 178-authority screening
report remains unchanged and is still replayed.

## Component review

The pinned RA workbooks state GBP thousands (Part 1 and Part 2, data sheet A3); inspection of their cells and notes found no tie rounding rule, so total and parish each use the closed interval [minus GBP 500, plus GBP 500].
RA own is RA total less RA parish; its error inherits both half thousand pound bounds.
Table 10 own is independently printed in whole pounds, adding a half pound bound: GBP 500 + GBP 500 + GBP 0.50 = GBP 1,000.50, closed.
The own difference is the total difference less the parish difference plus the retained Table 10 total less parish less own identity difference.
Own is an assertion: failure while total and parish pass stops the audit as a fixture error, never an exclusion.

No figure is adjusted or carried between years. Precision compatibility does not
prove rounding caused a difference. The model calculates changes from the published baseline.

Burnley remains excluded. RA Part 1 `RA_LA_Data_2026-27` HB54 is 8,832 thousand
pounds and EY54 is 222 thousand pounds. Table 10 `Data_Billing` H49 is 8,832,026
pounds, L49 is 222,800 pounds and N49 is 8,609,226 pounds. Total difference is
minus 26 pounds, parish difference minus 800 pounds and own difference 774
pounds. RA Part 2 row 54, SG row 53 and both annual Table 8c F81 cells supply no
explanatory adjustment. The annual own charges agree with Table 10, but that
does not resolve the parish discrepancy; the own difference now passes its derived bound.

The remaining failed component screens receive the same review: their raw rows
and note markers in RA Parts 1 and 2, SG, Table 10 and both annual workbooks are
retained in the manifest. No attributable explanation was established for an
out-of-precision difference. No numerical coincidence with another ledger line
is accepted as an explanation. Resolving the disagreement between the RA and
tax returns is the principal outstanding source need.

Exclusion counts overlap and cover all available returns, including note 4
cases which the earlier screen stopped before comparing components:

There are 99 distinct excluded councils. Every excluded row has a plain

## Notes and prior-year comparability

All authority-specific note markers are resolved to the full text and physical
note cell. RA Part 1 note 8 changes the retained business rates interpretation,
SG note 3 identifies double counted grants, and Lewes has figures still pending
approval in the held edition. Those councils remain excluded. RA Part 2 notes
1 and 2 concern reserve stocks, which the pilot does not use; the signed RA
Part 1 movements remain fixed. Those stock notes are reviewed without changing
any value. Rounded percentage notes do not affect the exact own-charge inputs.
Named referendum notes are retained, but the final report supplies the exact
threshold rather than the summaries' wording about increases "up to" a value.

Medway's revised prior own charge agrees in both held sources. The revised
North Kesteven inputs also agree, but its component discrepancy still excludes
it. No old value is carried forward. Table 10 `Data_Billing` A2 expressly says
that no authorities reorganise in 2026/27; both annual classes and both prior
own-charge cells are checked for every council. This uses the held tax source,
not the registry's held reorganisation field.

City of London and Isles of Scilly are checked individually. Both are named in
Annex B (pages 11 and 14); Scilly is also explicitly in Annex A paragraph 1(1)(b),
page 6. City's own charge includes its police function; annual Table 8d note d
says that element is not distinguishable. No police charge is added. Both years
use the same own-charge basis. City fails the parish component;
Scilly fails the total component. Neither receives a playable page.

The former own check was the only exclusion reason for 27 councils, not 22.
All 27 now pass: 22 had passed the original screen and five had been admitted
by note review before the former own check excluded them. Thus 170 + 27 = 197.
Those five are Warrington, North Somerset, Tonbridge and Malling, Trafford and
Gateshead. The correct original-screen reconciliation is 178 + 19 = 197.
All other exclusion decisions are unchanged; the manifest records every case.
All 170 previously eligible pilot records are unchanged, including source cells,
ledger inputs, reconciliation differences and exact freeze amounts.

Hull (`E06000010`) has RA total less Table 10 of minus GBP 500, parish zero,

## Referendum rules

The full report and both annexes were read. Rules are for 2026/27 only and apply
to the council's own relevant basic amount, excluding local precepts. Membership
of the ordinary category requires the Annex B name; shire districts require the
report's definition and the held separate county link. Named exceptions are
checked individually, including excluded councils in `reviewedPrinciple`.

Trafford and Warrington now carry 7.5%; North Somerset carries 9%.
Worcestershire shares the last principle but is a county and outside the 296.
The report's police, fire, combined authority and GLA principles are outside the
own billing-authority model. None is substituted for a billing-authority rule.
There are no unmapped surviving councils and no OR pair in the chosen cohort.
All five percentage principles have penny tests below, at and above the
threshold. Fractional penny thresholds also cover 5% of GBP 1,731.05 and
3% of GBP 123.45, where the strict GBP 5 condition decides. The district pound threshold has the same three checks and tests
where each of its two conditions holds alone. The interface permits crossing
the trigger and says that a referendum would be required, not that it is a cap.

## Arithmetic and inspection

Model `budget-freeze 0.2` retains integer units of GBP 0.0001, own Band D in
pence, and the base in hundredths of a Band D equivalent. Grants, reserves,
other financing, non-service adjustments, education, public health, net income
and published zeros remain fixed. The prototype range remains zero to twice
each positive published amount. Exact balance is tested before display rounding.

Ten raw-cell sets were inspected, two per class. The independent replay is
`python3 scripts/check-c2-decimal.py`: it reads the physical raw cells, checks
GSS, preserves inspected inputs, uses Python Decimal, compares each component
and checks the exact result against the JavaScript fixture. Its ten-row output
is included in the PR body. The current and prior annual row addresses are
independent; for example Camden is G4 in the current table and G5 in the prior.

The final PR body records the verification results. The production number
comparison is against an independently built archive of origin/main at the merge.
Main was merged as `5636d02`; only the state note conflicted and was replaced here. No public route, navigation, canonical,
sitemap, published figure, release constant or Estate Kit file is changed.
