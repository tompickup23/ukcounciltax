## Rules that matter

Never add a selected parish charge to an area average already containing average parishes. A council's own share excluding local precepts may still contain averaged special expenses. Police and general functions of the same combined authority can share a body code but be distinct source slots; do not deduplicate by code. Do not add police/fire/ASC again if bundled. Missing is not zero. Some official parish precepts are negative. Historical name matches are not proof of continuity.

Keep scenarios separate from published observations. No postcode storage or postcode-bearing share URLs. Recheck source/project guides before editing donor projects. Preserve private datasets and credentials; use existing CLI/environment authentication without printing secret values.
