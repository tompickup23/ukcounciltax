## Separate existing content issue

UK Council Tax still renders the registry's July Lancashire reorganisation
note, including the phrase `government-decided four-unitary model`. This was
confirmed in current main, the live Burnley profile and the review build.
The same note occurs in the registry entries for all 14 Lancashire councils.

UK Councils' `CLAUDE.md`, under Held data, explicitly withholds that registry
field pending correction at UK Places. UK Council Tax's profile template
still renders it directly. PR 67 does not introduce or alter this behaviour.
Treat this as a separate priority content correction, rather than a finding
against the new nearby links. Use the existing UK Councils hold as the
starting point; do not invent replacement reorganisation wording or hand-edit
the shared registry. This review did not revalidate the current legal or
policy position and makes no new claim about it.
